The House

One day, we'll make our own tequila.

That day is finally here.

Galindo is the tequila of a family distillery in the highlands of Los Altos de Jalisco. The story began in the mid-1800s, when Don Justo Galindo first cultivated agave in the region. In 1915 the family opened its first distillery — and generations later, every bottle is still crafted from 100% blue Weber agave.
