The House

One day, we'll make our own tequila.

That day is finally here.

Galindo is the tequila of a family distillery in the highlands of Los Altos de Jalisco. The story began in the mid-1800s, when Don Justo Galindo first cultivated agave in the region. In 1915 the family opened its first distillery — and generations later, every bottle is still crafted from 100% blue Weber agave.

Los Altos de Jalisco

Born in the
red highlands.

Our agave grows slowly in the iron-rich, red volcanic earth of the Jalisco highlands, more than 2,000 metres above the sea. The thin mountain air and cool nights coax out a sweeter, rounder agave — the quiet secret behind every bottle of Galindo.

The Craft

From agave to glass

There are no shortcuts in the highlands. Each bottle is the work of patient hands, slow fire and long years in oak.

  1. 01

    The Jima

    Mature agave is hand-harvested by jimadores, who strip each plant down to its heart — the piña — with a razor-sharp coa.

  2. 02

    Slow-cooked

    The piñas are slow-baked in the highland tradition until their starches soften into a deep, honeyed sweetness.

  3. 03

    Fermentation

    The roasted agave is crushed and its juices naturally fermented, where the raw character of the spirit first comes alive.

  4. 04

    Twice distilled

    Distilled a second time for a cleaner, more refined spirit — bright, smooth and unmistakably agave.

  5. 05

    Rested in oak

    From a nine-month Reposado to a five-year Reserva Privada, each expression rests quietly in oak beneath Casa Galindo.
