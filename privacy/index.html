Privacy

Updated 1 November 2026 · Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

We keep this short and plain. If anything here is unclear, ask us: we're happy to explain.

Who we are

Heroes of Agave (Pty) Ltd ("we", "Galindo") looks after Tequila Galindo in South Africa, a Tequila Heroes house. We decide what happens to the details you give us on tequilaheroes.com and at our events.

Questions, a copy of your details, corrections, or "please delete me / take my photo down":
info@tequilaheroes.com

What we collect

  • When you sign up (thank-you page, photo booth, events, or with one of our team): your name, mobile and/or email, and anything you choose to tell us, like your favourite Galindo.
  • Photo booth: only the photos you choose to send us. Taking a photo happens on your phone; nothing leaves it unless you tap Send to the Galindo album.
  • Your Galindo Member ID: the ID, your members' price status, and which album photos you open or save.
  • Visiting our website: anonymous visit counts (which pages, roughly where from) through Cloudflare Web Analytics. No cookies, and nothing that identifies you.
  • When you buy: what you bought, when, the price and how you paid (we never see or keep card numbers).

Why

  • To give you what you asked for: your Member ID, your photos, members' prices and your orders.
  • To send you news about releases and events, only if you ticked yes. Opt out any time: reply STOP or email us.
  • To keep proper sales records with Creative Beverages, our South African distributor, who invoices every sale.

Photo booth photos

When you send us a booth photo, you agree we may keep it and share it with Galindo members (in the members' album) and on our social media, and you confirm everyone in it is happy with that. Our team checks every photo before anyone else sees it. Want one taken down? Email us and we'll remove it.

Who sees your details

  • Our small Galindo team.
  • Creative Beverages, for orders and invoices.
  • The services that host our website and database. Your details may be stored on servers outside South Africa, with providers that protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA.

We never sell your details or give them to anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep them

While you're part of the Galindo community, or until you ask us to delete them. Sales records are kept as long as tax law requires (usually five years).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to how we use them, or withdraw your consent, at any time, by emailing us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled something, you can complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za.

Galindo is for adults: everything here is for people 18 and over.
